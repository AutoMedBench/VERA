---
name: med-report
description: Produce medical imaging reports from supplied observations or images with finding-level traceability and benchmark-compatible structure.
---

Preserve study identity and distinguish positive findings, pertinent negatives,
uncertainty, and comparison information. Keep findings consistent with the
impression and avoid claims unsupported by inputs. When a pilot tool is
available, use its authenticated output for iteration and retain its receipt.
Validate required sections and case coverage before submission.
